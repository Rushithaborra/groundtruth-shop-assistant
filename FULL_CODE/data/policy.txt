Returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of us receiving the returned item. Earbuds and other in-ear products cannot be returned once opened.

Exchanges
Apparel and footwear can be exchanged for a different size within 15 days of delivery, subject to availability. Exchange pickups are free of charge.

Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days across India. Orders above ₹999 ship free; for smaller orders a flat shipping fee of ₹49 applies. We do not ship outside India. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹5,000.

Warranty
Electronics carry a manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery; the warranty period is listed on each product. Footwear and apparel have no warranty but are covered by the returns policy. The warranty does not cover physical damage or water damage.

Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are dispatched. Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery.
